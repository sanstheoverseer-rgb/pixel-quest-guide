Base stat formulas (Wis→Mana regen, Vit→HP regen, Def/Atk/Dex%) are creator-sourced, not wiki-documented — the wiki has no central mechanics page. Weapon/gear numbers are pulled from wiki.playpixelquest.com. Damage scaling is additive: a weapon's own scaling stat (if it has one) adds flat damage on top of its base damage, and ATK's universal +1%/point then multiplies that whole total — same for every weapon, whether or not it has its own scaling stat. The DPS number is a real 30-second tick-by-tick simulation: mana starts full and regens from Wisdom, your secondary only fires when its cooldown is up and mana can afford it (otherwise it sits idle and the sim shows how many casts got skipped), flag-type secondaries only grant their stat bonus while actually active between casts, and mana-gated weapons (AWP, Musket line, etc.) can stall their own attacks. The "Shots" column is how many projectiles a weapon fires per attack as its basic pattern (staff twin-shots, 3-arrow bow spreads, boomerang double-hits) — damage is per-projectile and this multiplies it, assuming every one connects (true vs a dummy). Conditional/random extra projectiles (procs, "if Heavy Armor", stat thresholds) stay in the notes. The "Extra dps" column is a catch-all: for burn / frost / bleed it's a flat add (those don't stack — re-hitting only refreshes), for poison it's the per-stack DPS and the sim stacks it (every hit adds an instance, so live stacks ramp to fire-rate × duration then hold — the breakdown shows the stack count). Secondary projectiles that land with every hit (homing bursts, explosions, "on shoot fire N more") are their own line, "secHitDmg per hit", and scale with ATK% like normal damage. Armor/accessory that inflict a status (Stygian Plate's Sword poison, Necrotoxin) are folded in too, gated by any weapon-type requirement. A weapon's own "on equip" stat bonus (Laevateinn's +10 ATK, etc.) is always-on and shown in the effective-stat readout. Still not modeled: "On Rallied" bonuses on weapons/accessories, summoned pets (Ashen Warrior, Octosword orbit), and ability/bomb damage scaling with ATK. Health-threshold and status-based armor passives (e.g. "below 25% HP, heal") stay in each item's notes as informational only. Doesn't model target defense.
